Appendix C. Business Rules Register

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Rule**   **Module (Scope)**           **Rule text**
  ---------- ---------------------------- --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  BR-01      Accounts                     A parent can use the app alone (Solo Mode) and still keeps a full record.

  BR-02      Accounts                     Every invite and every attempt to reach the co-parent is logged with date and time.

  BR-05      Records                      Data export, tone, safety and reporting tools are available to every user.

  BR-06      Notifications                A message that stays unread moves from push to SMS and email. The waiting time is to be set.

  BR-07      Notifications                Sent, delivered and read status is saved in the record.

  BR-08      Messaging                    A sent message cannot be edited or deleted.

  BR-09      Messaging                    Tone feedback is shown only to the sender.

  BR-10      Messaging                    A held-back incoming message can be opened by the user. The reason it was flagged is shown, and the original stays in the record.

  BR-11      Messaging, Safety            AI is opt-in. The app does not train on user messages. AI suggestions never enter the legal record.

  BR-12      Calendar                     Every approval needs a confirm step and has a short undo window. The log records who approved, when, and from which device.

  BR-13      Calendar                     Original event details are kept. An event that has already started cannot be deleted.

  BR-14      Expenses                     A reimbursement request shows its deadline and the proof needed.

  BR-15      Expenses                     Once a split rule from the order is entered, it applies to every shared expense.

  BR-16      Child Support Sync           Agency values are read-only in the app. Each one shows its source (API or batch) and the time it was last updated.

  BR-17      Child Support Sync           A difference between the agency record and a parent-recorded payment is flagged, never changed automatically.

  BR-18      Child Support Sync           Linking a case needs an identity check and recorded consent. The parent can unlink.

  BR-19      Child Support Sync           Nothing is sent to the agency without the parent\'s confirmation, and only where the agency allows it.

  BR-20      Records                      Every certified export carries a record ID that anyone can check on a public verification page.

  BR-21      Records                      Records tied to a case can be put on legal hold. Other data can be deleted on request, after the user is offered an export.

  BR-22      Safety                       In Safety Mode, address and location are hidden, check-ins are off, contact details are masked, and notifications are discreet.

  BR-23      Safety, Child Support Sync   Where the agency allows it, a family violence flag on the case can offer Safety Mode to the parent.

  BR-24      Professionals                A professional gets read-only access to what the parent chooses to share.

  BR-25      Support                      Every request gets a case number. Issues tied to court dates are handled first.

  BR-26      ---                          The web app carries the full feature set. The mobile apps are built on the same core.

  BR-27      Child Support Sync           Batch data is loaded only for cases a parent has linked. Records for any other case are discarded and not stored.

  BR-28      Child Support Sync           If an API call or batch load fails, the app keeps the last good data and marks it as out of date. It never shows a blank or guessed value.

  BR-29      Child Support Sync           Every batch file is checked before it is loaded. A file that fails the check is held back and reported to support staff.

  BR-30      Child Support Sync           Where a state offers both channels and they give different values, the newer agency value is shown and both are logged.

  BR-31      Support                      Support staff never contact the co-parent about the case of a parent, and never confirm to the co-parent that a case exists.
