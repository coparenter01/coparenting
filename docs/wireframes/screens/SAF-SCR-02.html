Before you turn on Safety Mode

Safety Mode hides some details from the other parent and makes alerts discreet. The Exit now button leaves this site quickly, but it does not delete your browser history and does not stop spyware. If you can, use a safer device. If you are in danger, call 911 if it is safe to do so, or call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233 (TTY 1-800-787-3224).
