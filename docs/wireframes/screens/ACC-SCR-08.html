How will you use ParentPath?

You can change this later. If your co-parent joins after you start alone, your household becomes Linked and your records stay complete.

Invite my co-parent Send an invite by email, text message or a link you share. You both share messages, the calendar and expenses. Invite co-parent → Use Solo Mode Keep a full record on your own. You can still message a co-parent who is not on the app by text or email. Choose Solo Mode →
Privacy: records you save in Solo Mode stay visible only to you after your co-parent joins, until you choose to share them.
