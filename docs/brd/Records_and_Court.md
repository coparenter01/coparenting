## 2. Purpose and scope

**2.1 Purpose.** This module turns what parents do in the app into records a court can review. It covers certified exports, proof that an export is genuine, support for affidavits and subpoenas, notes on court acceptance, model order language, clear rules on how long data is kept, and a journal for incident notes.

**2.2 Objectives this module supports.** Objective IDs come from Common BRD section 2.3.

| ID     | How this module supports it                                                                                                                                                |
|--------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| OBJ-01 | Certified exports with a record ID and a public check page are the court-ready record of messages, calendar and expenses (REC-01).                                         |
| OBJ-02 | A parent can select agency data for an export. The hearing packet export of Child Support Sync uses the same certified export (REC-01).                                    |
| OBJ-03 | Fixes the High-rated pain point P16 in the first release (REC-01, REC-05) and with Phase 2 (REC-02, REC-03). Also addresses the Medium-rated P17 and P18 (REC-04, REC-06). |
| OBJ-04 | Data export is never behind a paywall (REC-05), and the free core plan includes records and exports (Scope document decision 9).                                           |
| OBJ-05 | Deletion and legal hold rules are plain and published (REC-06). Exports follow the Safety Mode hiding rules (FR-REC-01-03).                                                |

**2.3 In scope.** The seven features REC-01 to REC-07 listed in section 5.

**2.4 Out of scope for this BRD.** These items belong to another module or to a later decision.

## 3. Users and roles

Roles are defined in Common BRD 7.1 and the shared permission matrix is in Common BRD 7.2. The table shows what each role does in this module.

| Role                                              | What they do in this module                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|---------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Parent                                            | Makes exports, keeps journal notes, asks for a business records affidavit, asks for deletion of non-record data, and asks for a legal hold.                                                                      |
| Co-parent                                         | The same as the Parent, once linked. Each parent exports only what they can see.                                                                                                                                 |
| Professional                                      | Reads the exports and journal notes that a parent shares (Common BRD 7.2). Cannot post. Access comes with PRO-01, Phase 2. Whether a professional can ask for an affidavit is open (Q-19).                       |
| Support staff                                     | Handles affidavit requests by hand. Sees the request details, the Record ID, the page count, the record types and the date range. Cannot read parent messages or open the content of an export (Common BRD 7.1). |
| Platform admin                                    | Runs the system. Cannot edit or delete a record. Access is logged (Common BRD 7.1).                                                                                                                              |
| Court, agency staff, attorney or any other person | Not users of the app. They check a Record ID on the public verification page and read the public pages (court notes, model order language, retention policy, records request page).                              |
| Third party and Child                             | No feature in this module. Common BRD 7.2 gives a Third party no access to records and exports.                                                                                                                  |

Permissions for module data: Common BRD 7.2 gives the Parent and the Co-parent CR on records and exports, the Professional R (shared only), and support staff and a Platform admin no access. Handling an affidavit request needs a limited right for support staff that Common BRD 7.2 does not give. A journal note is private to the parent who wrote it. CR-01 asks to add the rows for affidavit requests and journal notes. The legal and operations process for subpoenas, and the person who produces records on a subpoena, are open (Q-17).

## 12. Dependencies and assumptions

**12.1 This module needs** (Scope document 7.9): Messaging, Calendar and Parenting Time, Expenses and Payments, Notifications (delivery logs), and Child Support Sync (the hearing packet). This BRD adds Accounts and Onboarding (roles, the contact-attempt log and account closure), Billing and Plans (plan and storage limit), Professionals and Access (shared items), Support (affidavit requests) and Children and Documents (the children in the household).

**12.2 Modules that need this module** (Common BRD section 4).

| Module                   | What it needs from Records and Court                                           |
|--------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Accounts and Onboarding  | The export before account closure, and the legal hold (ACC-12)                 |
| Child Support Sync       | The certified export for the hearing packet (CSS-11)                           |
| Professionals and Access | The exports and journal notes that a parent shares                             |
| Support                  | Affidavit, deletion and hold requests to handle                                |
| Billing and Plans        | The definition of notarized records as a paid extra, and exports that are free |

The Common BRD build order places Records after Expenses and before Child Support Sync.

**12.3 Assumptions**

- The first release is a web app. Mobile follows (Scope document 4.4).

- Module names are working names (Scope document 4.4).

- The phase for each feature is a proposal, not a commitment (Scope document 4.4).

- Access levels for roles are proposals (Scope document 5).

- Screens and messages are in English in the MVP (Accounts BRD, decision Q-01).

- Courts decide what they accept. The words court-ready record are used and the words court approved are not (Scope document decision 7).

- Legal review of the exports, the certification statement, the court notes, the affidavit and subpoena process, the retention and deletion rules and the terms of use is done before launch (Scope document decision 7).

- The pages of other apps named in the market review describe those apps on October 13, 2026 and can change.

- Pain point P17 rests on user reviews that are not verified (Pain Point workbook).
