## 2. Purpose and scope

**2.1 Purpose.** This module protects parents who are at risk and builds trust in how the app treats personal data. No app in the review lists a dedicated safety mode, so this is a real gap (Scope document 7.12). The module gives Safety Mode for survivors of abuse, controls for alert previews and sensitive images, and one place where a parent controls the AI features and sees what they read.

**2.2 Objectives this module supports.** Objective IDs come from Common BRD section 2.3.

| ID     | How this module supports it                                                                                                                                                          |
|--------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| OBJ-02 | A family violence flag on a child support case can offer Safety Mode to the parent, where the agency allows it (SAF-01, with CSS-08 in Sync phase 2).                                |
| OBJ-03 | Fixes the High-rated pain point P14 in the first release (SAF-01, SAF-02) and addresses the Medium-rated P15 (SAF-03). P13 puts the safety tools in every plan (Billing BRD BIL-04). |
| OBJ-04 | Safety Mode, the alert and image controls and the AI controls are in every plan, including the free one (BR-05).                                                                     |
| OBJ-05 | This is the main objective of the module: protect parents at risk, and be plain about how AI and personal data are used.                                                             |

**2.3 In scope.** The three features SAF-01 to SAF-03 listed in section 5.

**2.4 Out of scope for this BRD.** These items belong to another module or to a later decision.

| Item                                                                       | Where it belongs                                                                                    |
|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| The tone check, the incoming message shield and the report tool themselves | Messaging (MSG-04, MSG-06, MSG-14). This module holds their switches and their privacy notices.     |
| Sending alerts, and the wording of alerts                                  | Notifications (NTF). This module sets the preview level that Notifications follows.                 |
| Check-ins at exchanges                                                     | Calendar and Parenting Time (CAL-09, Phase 3). It is not offered in Safety Mode.                    |
| The fee-waiver and safety access request, and free access for survivors    | Billing and Plans (BIL-05, Phase 2) and Support. Safety Mode itself is free in every plan (BIL-04). |
| The family violence flag and the case link                                 | Child Support Sync (CSS-08, Sync phase 2). This module shows the offer.                             |
| The AI features themselves                                                 | Messaging (MSG-05, MSG-11, MSG-13) and Expenses and Payments (EXP-11).                              |
| Choosing the advocacy partner and the panel of survivors                   | Manager (Scope document decision 6).                                                                |
| A crisis line or any live help                                             | Not in this BRD. The app shows links to hotlines that advocates approve.                            |
| Protection against spyware or a hacked device                              | Not in this BRD. The app tells the user that this is a risk (decision Q-02).                        |
| Safety Mode for the mobile apps                                            | Mobile phase.                                                                                       |
| Legal advice                                                               | Out of scope for now (Scope document 4.2).                                                          |
| Agency staff as users                                                      | Out of scope for now (Scope document 4.2, decision 13).                                             |

**2.5 Release phases of this module.** MVP (web): SAF-01, SAF-02 and SAF-03. The offer of Safety Mode from a family violence flag is part of SAF-01 and comes in Sync phase 2 (FR-SAF-01-17). This matches the Scope document, which lists 3 MVP features for this module. The phase of each feature is a proposal (Scope document 4.4).

## 3. Users and roles

Roles are defined in Common BRD 7.1 and the shared permission matrix is in Common BRD 7.2. The table shows what each role does in this module.

| Role                                           | What they do in this module                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
|------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Parent                                         | Turns Safety Mode on and off for their own account, sets the alert preview level and the image control, and controls the AI features. Sees the AI use log.                                                                                                      |
| Co-parent                                      | The same as the Parent, once linked. Does not see that the other parent uses Safety Mode. Sees only that some details are hidden until shared.                                                                                                                  |
| Support staff                                  | Handles the safety access request of Billing and Plans, whose basis shows only to staff and to the parent (Billing BRD FR-BIL-05-10). Cannot see whether a user has Safety Mode on, cannot read parent messages (Common BRD 7.1) and cannot see the AI use log. |
| Platform admin                                 | Runs the system. Access is logged (Common BRD 7.1). Cannot turn Safety Mode on or off for a user.                                                                                                                                                               |
| Professional                                   | Sees what a parent shares, with the same hiding rules as the parent (Professionals BRD FR-PRO-01-10).                                                                                                                                                           |
| Domestic violence advocates and survivor panel | Review and approve the design. They are not users of the app (Scope document decision 6).                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Agency staff                                   | Not users of the app (Scope document 4.2, decision 13).                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Third party and Child                          | No feature in this module.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

Permissions for module data: Common BRD 7.2 has no row for the Safety Mode setting, the alert and image settings or the AI settings. Each setting belongs to the user, and only that user can read or change it. The Safety Mode setting is never shown to the co-parent, to a professional, to support staff or to a third party. CR-01 asks to add the rows.

- Module names are working names (Scope document 4.4).

- The phase for each feature is a proposal, not a commitment (Scope document 4.4).

- Access levels for roles are proposals (Scope document 5).

- Screens and messages are in English in the MVP (Accounts BRD, decision Q-01).

- Safety Mode is open to every parent from day one and needs no flag (Scope document decision 5).

- Safety Mode is not released without the approval of domestic violence advocates and a panel of survivors (Scope document decision 6).

- Safety Mode cannot protect a user from spyware or from a person who can use the device of the user. The safety notice says so.

- The NNEDV pages named in the review describe its guidance on October 14, 2026 and can change.

- P15 is rated Medium, and P14 is rated High (Pain Point workbook).
