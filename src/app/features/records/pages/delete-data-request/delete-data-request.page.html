Data Deletion & Legal Retention Disclosure

In accordance with Family Court compliance standards, when you submit a deletion request:

  • Deleted: Account login sessions, cached media drafts, local client preferences, and non-certified temporary work files.
  • Kept: Certified export logs, court-admissible message threads, parenting schedule history, financial ledger transactions, and audit records. These records are held for the mandatory retention window (7 years or until all minor children reach age 26).

We strongly recommend downloading an authoritative export archive before requesting deletion.
