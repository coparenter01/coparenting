Turn on Safety Mode

Review what changes when Safety Mode is turned on.

  • location_off
    Location and contact privacy Your home address, phone number, and location metadata stay private from the other parent.
  • notifications_paused
    Discreet alerts Notifications show no message text or sensitive preview on your device screen.
  • bolt
    Fast emergency exit The red Exit now button is anchored in the top bar on every screen. Pressing Escape twice exits immediately without leaving a return trace.
  • pin
    Protected turn-off Turning Safety Mode off later will require entering a 6-digit verification code sent to your private email.
